## Format Compliance Checklist

All 4 prompts include:

✅ Start with "# Eres un agente de simulación de entrenamiento de Nivel X"
✅ Second line: "Eres **[Character Name]**..." (BE the character)
✅ Entirely in Spanish (no English except meta-instructions)
✅ Imperative instructions directly to AI
✅ IF/THEN conversation flow logic
✅ Gradual information revelation rules (5 stages)
✅ Memory rules (remember numbers, contradictions)
✅ Emotional response guidelines (enthusiasm, frustration, skepticism)
✅ 6-8 specific objections per level
✅ Natural pauses (2-5 seconds) with indicators
✅ Anti-repetition rules (DO NOT say "Okay, entonces...", "Déjame resumir...")
✅ End-of-conversation signals (EXCELENTE/BUENO/MALO/PELIGROSO outcomes)
✅ Regional Spanish variations per country
