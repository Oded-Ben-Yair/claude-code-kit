## What Was Done

### Grok Bullish Bias Fix (Combined Approach 1+3)

**Problem**: Grok predicts bullish 55.7% of the time at 40% weight with highest avg confidence (0.395). Alone produces consensus_score = 0.174, which is 2x the 0.08 threshold. System bullish accuracy: 22.7% (worst direction).

**Solution**: Combined two approaches:

1. **Asymmetric Threshold** (Approach 1): Separate bull/bear thresholds
   - Bullish threshold raised: 0.08 -> 0.15 (env-var tunable: `BULL_THRESHOLD`)
   - Bearish threshold unchanged: 0.08 (env-var tunable: `BEAR_THRESHOLD`)
   - Adaptive scaling preserved: `adaptive_bull = bull_threshold * (1 - disagreement * 0.15)`

2. **Category-Specific Weight Reduction** (Approach 3): Reduce Grok weight where it underperforms
   - Indices 24h: grok 0.40 -> 0.25, claude 0.30 (new highest)
   - Stocks 24h: grok 0.40 -> 0.30
   - Commodities 24h: grok 0.40 -> 0.30
   - Fallback 24h: grok 0.40 -> 0.30
   - Forex 24h: UNCHANGED at 0.40 (69% accuracy, Grok strongest)
   - Crypto 24h: UNCHANGED at 0.35

3. **Gate-Level Safety Net**: direction_bullish penalty raised +0.05 -> +0.20 in pocket_thresholds.py
